## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## 1. Proposed Solution
Our proposed solution is a digital marketplace that directly connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers and bulk buyers without depending on multiple intermediaries.

The platform allows farmers to list their agricultural products, update prices, manage stock and receive orders directly from customers.

Key Features:
- Direct buying and selling between farmers and consumers.
- Online product listing with quantity and price details.
- Bulk ordering facility for shops, hotels and supermarkets.
- AI-based demand forecasting to predict future product requirements.
- Smart logistics support for efficient product delivery.
- Transparent pricing to ensure fair earnings for farmers.
- Order tracking and secure online payments.
Innovation and Uniqueness:

The main innovation is combining a direct agricultural marketplace with AI-based demand prediction and route optimization. This helps reduce unnecessary intermediaries, minimize transportation costs and improve the overall agricultural supply chain.
## 2. Technical Approach
Technologies Used:
HTML-Webpage structure,
CSS-Website design,
JavaScript-Interactive features,
Python-Backend development,
Django-Web application framework,
SQLite-Database management,
Machine Learning-Demand forecasting,
Google Maps API-Location and route support.

Implementation Methodology:
1. Develop a user-friendly website for farmers and buyers.
2. Create separate registration and login facilities.
3. Allow farmers to upload products, prices and available quantities.
4. Enable consumers and bulk buyers to browse and purchase products.
5. Store product, user and order details in the database.
6. Implement AI-based demand forecasting using previous sales data.
7. Integrate route optimization to identify efficient delivery routes.
8. Provide order tracking and payment facilities.

## 3. Feasibility and Viability
The proposed solution is technically and economically feasible because it uses commonly available technologies and internet-based services.

Feasibility:
- The platform can be developed using open-source technologies such as Python, Django and SQLite.
- Farmers and buyers can access the platform using smartphones or computers.
- Existing digital payment systems can be integrated.
- AI models can be developed using historical sales and demand data.
Potential Challenges:
1. Limited internet access in rural areas.
2. Farmers may have difficulty using digital platforms.
3. Maintaining product quality during transportation.
4. Managing changes in agricultural prices.
5. Predicting demand accurately during seasonal changes.
Solutions to Overcome Challenges:
- Provide a simple and user-friendly interface.
- Support local languages for easy communication.
- Provide training and assistance for farmers.
- Partner with local delivery services.
- Regularly update demand prediction models using new sales data.
## 4. Impact and Benefits
The proposed digital marketplace provides several benefits to farmers, consumers and the agricultural industry.

Economic Benefits:
- Farmers can earn better prices by reducing intermediaries.
- Consumers can purchase products at competitive prices.
- Bulk buyers can directly purchase agricultural products.
- Transportation and operational costs can be reduced.
Social Benefits:
- Supports small-scale farmers and rural communities.
- Improves digital awareness among farmers.
- Creates better connections between farmers and customers.
- Provides equal market opportunities for small farmers.
Environmental Benefits:
- Reduces unnecessary transportation.
- Helps minimize food wastage through demand forecasting.
- Improves delivery efficiency using optimized routes.
- Supports sustainable agricultural distribution.
Overall Impact:

This solution creates a transparent and efficient agricultural marketplace where farmers receive fair value for their products and consumers get fresh agricultural products at reasonable prices.
